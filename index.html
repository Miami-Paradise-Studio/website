How the studio decides

A small R&D team running Build-Measure-Learn cycles. Every idea ships as a Minimum Viable Experiment, and every experiment has a number attached before it starts.

  1. 01

    Lean MVE engine

    Hypothesis, KPI, experiment, decision. Data beats opinion, including ours.

  2. 02

    Actionable metrics

    D1 retention, funnel conversion, core loop completion. Green scales, red gets pivoted or killed in the same week.

  3. 03

    Marketing in parallel

    Community, wishlists and social validation run alongside the build, not after it.

  4. 04

    AI acceleration

    Aggressive use of AI and existing assets to shorten the distance to signal. Speed is the only advantage a small team has.
