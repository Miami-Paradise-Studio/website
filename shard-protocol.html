SHARD Protocol

Team-based 4v4 extraction PvP. Capture three flags, breach the enemy base, silence the defence, with tactical voice commands.

4v4 teams 3 classes 5 voice systems PC first

How a match runs

A match, in three gates

One win condition. No score creep, no comeback mechanic that erases the last ten minutes.

01

Capture flags

Secure the control points across the middle. Every flag taken weakens the enemy defence and opens a route that was closed a minute ago.

02

Breach the base

With the flags held, the stronghold opens. This is where voice commands stop being convenience and start being the difference.

03

Extract

Clear the remaining defenders and hold the extraction point. First team through all three gates takes the round.

Your voice is a weapon

Five command systems that change the battlefield. No binding stack to memorise. Speak, and the round reacts.
